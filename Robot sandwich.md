# Robot sandwich Ham

## Assumptions 

- robot hand is empty
- all ingredints are present
- on a counter top with a butter knife

## steps

1. robot wash his hands
2. robot drys them with paper towel
3. robot grabs the bread, ham & mayo out the fridge
4. grabs a plate from the dishwasher
5. put the ingredints and plate on the counter top
6. robot open bread bag and get two slices of bread out and put the two slices on the plate
7. robot  close the bread bag and move to the ham
8. open the ham container and get two slices of meat out
9. put the 2 slices of meat on one slice of bread
10. robot close the ham container and put it back in the fridge
11. robot open the mayo jar and grabs a butter knife to spread it on the other piece of bread that has nothing on it
12. Robot spread the mayo on the bread and close the sandwhich
